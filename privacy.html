Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Snapp — MediaCleaner ("the App") handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

Snapp does not collect, store, or share any personal data such as:

  • Name, email address, or phone number
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • User accounts or passwords

2. Photo Library Access

The App requires access to your device’s photo library in order to:

  • Display your photos and videos for sorting
  • Allow you to mark media for deletion
  • Move items into albums
  • Delete selected items permanently (only with your confirmation)

All photo and video processing happens locally on your device. Snapp does not upload your media to any server.

3. Data Storage

The App stores only minimal technical information locally on your device, such as:

  • Which items were marked as checked
  • Which items were moved to the trash list
  • Statistics about deleted file sizes
  • Subscription/trial status

This data is saved only in Apple UserDefaults on your device and is never shared externally.

4. Subscriptions and Payments

Snapp offers optional premium subscriptions through Apple’s in-app purchase system (StoreKit). Payment information is handled directly by Apple.

The App does not collect or store any payment or credit card details.

5. Third-Party Services

Snapp does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or external tracking tools.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with anyone.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

8. Your Control

You can manage photo permissions anytime in:

Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Snapp

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at:

📩 support@yourapp.com

Snapp — MediaCleaner is designed to keep your media private. Everything stays on your device.
