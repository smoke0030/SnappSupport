# Snapp — MediaCleaner

Snapp is an iOS application designed to help users quickly clean up and organize their photo library.

The app allows you to swipe through photos and videos, mark unwanted items for deletion, and free up storage space safely and efficiently.

Snapp works entirely on your device and does not upload your media to any external servers.

## Privacy Policy

Snapp respects your privacy.

The application only accesses your Photo Library to provide its core functionality, including:

- Displaying photos and videos for sorting  
- Marking items for deletion  
- Deleting selected media only with user confirmation  
- Organizing content into albums  

Snapp does not collect personal data and does not use third-party analytics or tracking tools.
